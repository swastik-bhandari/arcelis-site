Research lab · Kathmandu, Nepal

Students do the research. Professors advise.

Arcelis Labs is a research lab in Kathmandu. Students work on research projects, and professors and researchers in Nepal or abroad advise them. Each project aims for a paper, code or data that others can use.

For professors Advise students on a question you care about. Remote advising works.

Open by default Code, data and results are released openly where possible.

How it works

From a question to a published result.

  1. 1

    Proposed

    A professor, researcher or student submits a project idea.

  2. 2

    Reviewed

    We check that it is feasible, has a committed mentor and a clear output.

  3. 3

    Open

    The project is listed here and students apply to join.

  4. 4

    Active

    The team works to a plan, with regular meetings and deadlines.

  5. 5

    Published

    Results are submitted to a venue, with code and data released openly where possible.

What we commit to

  • Every applicant gets a reply within 14 days.
  • Credit is clear from day one. Authorship follows contribution, and we agree on it before work starts.
  • Open by default. Code, data and benchmarks are released under open licenses whenever we can.
  • Mentors from anywhere. Professors can mentor remotely, from any university in the world.

Research focus

Research areas.

Our first projects are in language and computing. Proposals in other areas are welcome.

Low-resource NLP

Methods for languages with little digital text, starting with Nepali, Maithili, Tamang and Nepal Bhasa.

Benchmarks & evaluation

Open datasets and reproducible evaluations that show what models can and cannot do in these languages.

Efficient systems

Inference, kernels and HPC that let models run fast on modest hardware, offline and at the edge.
